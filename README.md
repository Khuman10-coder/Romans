The Roman Empire was known for its powerful military, advanced engineering, organized government, and rich culture. The Romans built a vast network of roads, bridges, aqueducts, and cities that connected different parts of the empire and improved trade and communication. Their army was highly disciplined and helped Rome expand across Europe, North Africa, and parts of Asia. Roman law and administration were also highly developed and later influenced many modern legal systems. The empire was famous for impressive architecture such as the Colosseum and large public baths and temples. 
During the Pax Romana, a long period of peace and stability, trade, literature, science, and art flourished. The Romans also spread the Latin language and played an important role in the growth of Christianity, which later became the empire official religion.
