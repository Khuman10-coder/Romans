MESSI

INTRO ABOUT THE GOAT

Lionel Messi is an Argentine professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players in history. Born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina, Messi's journey to football greatness began at a very young age. Despite being diagnosed with a growth hormone deficiency as a child, his exceptional talent caught the eye of FC Barcelona, who offered to pay for his medical treatment.

At Barcelona, Messi developed into a footballing phenomenon. His extraordinary dribbling, vision, and clinical finishing became his trademarks. He rose to global prominence during a trophy-laden career at FC Barcelona (2004-2021), where he broke countless records and established himself as the club's greatest player ever.

After Barcelona, Messi had a successful spell with Paris Saint-Germain (2021-2023), winning two league titles. Currently, he plays for Inter Miami in the MLS, where he continues to demonstrate his exceptional skills and bring unprecedented attention to American soccer.

The pinnacle of Messi's career came in 2022 when he captained Argentina to World Cup glory in Qatar, completing his trophy collection and cementing his legacy as one of football's greatest ever players. His creativity, consistency, and record-breaking achievements—including a record eight Ballon d'Or awards—have left an indelible mark on the sport.

Key Career Highlights:
• Made his Barcelona debut at age 17
• Became Barcelona's all-time top scorer
• Argentina's all-time top scorer
• Won the Olympic gold medal in 2008
• Led Argentina to Copa America victory in 2021
• Crowned World Cup champion in 2022

TROPHIES

Lionel Messi has amassed an incredible collection of trophies throughout his career:

With Barcelona:
• 10x La Liga titles
• 7x Copa del Rey
• 4x UEFA Champions League
• 3x FIFA Club World Cup

With PSG:
• 2x Ligue 1 titles

With Argentina:
• 1x FIFA World Cup (2022)
• 1x Copa América (2021)
• 1x Finalissima (2022)

Individual Awards:
• 8x Ballon d'Or
• 6x European Golden Shoe
• 2x FIFA The Best Award

RECORDS BROKEN

Messi has broken numerous records throughout his career:

• Most Ballon d'Or wins (8)
• Most goals in La Liga history (474)
• Most goals in a calendar year (91 goals in 2012)
• Most goals for a single club (672 for Barcelona)
• Most goals in El Clásico (26)
• Most assists in La Liga history (192)
• Most goals in Argentina national team history (104)
• First player to score in every minute of a game
• Most hat-tricks in La Liga (36)
• Most goals in a single La Liga season (50)
